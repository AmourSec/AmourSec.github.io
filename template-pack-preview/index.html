模板包预览素材计划

Codex Workflow Template Pack 的本地预览素材计划，包含封面文案、5 个可截图区块、说明文案和合规边界。

这个页面整理模板包的预览素材计划。

它只是本地准备材料：不生成下载入口，不发布产品，不上架平台，不设置价格，不收款，也不向任何第三方提交。

封面文案

元素 文案
标题 Codex Workflow Template Pack
副标题 Markdown templates for turning vague tasks into structured AI-ready briefs.
标签 Troubleshooting / Local Pages / Editing / Data Cleanup
边界说明 Local draft. No platform listing, payment link, or guaranteed outcome.

预览区块

区块 截图内容 图片说明 证明什么
文件树 README、TERMS、start-here 文件和 4 个工作流目录 A small Markdown pack organized by use case, with terms and boundaries visible from the start. 不是零散提示词，而是可检查的文件包
技术排查清单 目标、环境、命令、预期、实际结果、最终复核 Turns a vague “it failed” report into a structured debugging brief. 能减少技术求助前的来回追问
本地商家 intake 商家基础信息、服务、证明材料、边界 A simple intake for drafting a one-page business site without inventing claims. 可覆盖非技术小业务资料整理
内容润色流程 目标、受众、事实、避免声明、编辑步骤 An editing workflow that makes uncertainty, claims, and review duties explicit. 能把粗稿改成更清楚、更谨慎的发布材料
数据清理清单 数据目的、字段、常见问题、输出要求 A non-sensitive data cleanup checklist with change-log and manual-review expectations. 强调可追踪清理，不鼓励静默删除或隐私推断

Alt Text 草稿

  1. File tree showing README, TERMS, start-here files, and four workflow template folders.
  2. Technical troubleshooting checklist with goal, environment, command, expected result, actual result, and final review fields.
  3. Local business page intake template with fields for business basics, offer, proof, and boundaries.
  4. Content editing workflow template showing draft goal, audience, required facts, claims to avoid, and editing steps.
  5. Data cleaning checklist with dataset purpose, field definitions, known problems, and output requirements.
